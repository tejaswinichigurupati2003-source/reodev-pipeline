For one person's version

Open Interview guide on their card on the board. It fills in their name and company, picks their role's questions, and includes the invitation to send.

Ground rules

  • Never mention Zoom, an acquisition, or what any vendor will do. Ask about change in general and about their own past, so what you hear is evidence, not agreement.
  • Ask one open question at a time, then stay quiet. Ask "can you give a recent example?"
  • This is research, not a pitch. Ask permission before recording or quoting.
  • Write down their exact words for the insights document (claim, evidence, so-what).
